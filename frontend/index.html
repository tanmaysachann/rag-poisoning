INTERPRETATION

Integrity catches edits made after a trusted snapshot. Accepted-ingest misinformation needs content checks. The current detector catches overt query-matched insertion and misses most subtle answer substitutions. The learned answer selector improves exact alias match but answers more often when labeled support is removed. Real SLM SRQ was measured and failed as a standalone detector; the local 0.5B answer model also did not reliably produce cited answers. Both remain outside the live defense.
